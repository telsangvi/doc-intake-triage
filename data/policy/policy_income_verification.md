# Policy 4.2 — Income Verification

## 4.2(a) Requirement
Every benefit application must state the applicant's monthly household income.
An application with no income figure is incomplete and must be returned to the
applicant rather than assessed.

## 4.2(b) Acceptable evidence
Monthly household income may be evidenced by a salary slip, a bank statement,
or a signed declaration. An income proof must state the period the figure
covers, so that the monthly figure can be derived.

## 4.2(c) Thresholds
Household income above the published ceiling disqualifies an application from
the means-tested stream and must be routed for manual assessment instead of
being auto-routed.
