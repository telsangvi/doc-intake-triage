# Policy 5.4 — Medical Evidence

## 5.4(a) When required
Applications for a disability allowance must include medical evidence from a
treating provider.

## 5.4(b) Required content
Medical evidence must name the patient, name the treating provider, and state
the diagnosed condition. A letter missing any of these is incomplete and must
be returned for the missing detail.

## 5.4(c) Confidence and legibility
Where a medical document is illegible, or its fields cannot be read reliably,
the case must be routed to human review rather than auto-processed.
